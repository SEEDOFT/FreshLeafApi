# FreshLeaf Project Context

## Company Overview

FreshLeaf is a fresh produce delivery platform based in Cambodia, providing convenient online shopping for fresh vegetables, fruits, herbs, and other perishable goods.

## Service Area

- **Primary Location**: Phnom Penh
- **Coverage**: Surrounding provinces in Cambodia
- **Delivery**: Door-to-door delivery service

## Products

- Fresh vegetables
- Fresh fruits
- Fresh herbs
- Other perishable goods

## Payment Methods

- Cash on delivery (COD)
- Online payment (credit card, e-wallet)

## Operating Hours

- Online ordering: 24/7
- Customer support: Available during business hours

## Contact

- App-based customer support through AI chat
- In-app messaging for order inquiries
